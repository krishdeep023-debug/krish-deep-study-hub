A LETTER TO GOD

SEBA Class 10 English | Chapter Notes

1. About the Author

Gregorio López y Fuentes was a Mexican writer and journalist. He wrote about ordinary people and rural life.

2. Detailed Summary

Lencho was a hardworking farmer who lived with his family in a house on the top of a low hill. His crops depended on rainfall.

One day, Lencho saw dark clouds and expected rain. Soon rain began to fall. However, the rain turned into a terrible hailstorm. The hailstones destroyed his entire crop.

Lencho became worried because his family might face hunger. However, he had complete faith in God. He decided to write a letter to God requesting one hundred pesos to sow his field again and support his family.

The postmaster read the letter and was deeply impressed by Lencho's faith. He collected money from his employees and contributed some of his own money. Together they managed to send Lencho seventy pesos.

When Lencho received the money, he was not surprised. But he became angry because the amount was less than what he had requested. He believed that God could not make a mistake. He suspected that the post office employees had taken the remaining money.

Lencho wrote another letter to God asking for the remaining money. He also requested God not to send it through the post office because he believed the employees were dishonest.

3. Main Characters

Lencho

A hardworking farmer with strong faith in God. He is innocent and misunderstands the post office employees.

The Postmaster

A kind and generous man who helps Lencho by collecting money.

Post Office Employees

They contribute money to help Lencho and protect his faith in God.

4. Main Themes

  • Faith and belief in God.
  • Kindness and generosity.
  • Human misunderstanding.
  • Hope during difficult times.
  • Irony and innocence.

5. Important Questions and Answers

Q1. Who was Lencho?

Answer: Lencho was a hardworking farmer who lived with his family in a house on the top of a low hill.

Q2. Why did Lencho need rain?

Answer: Lencho needed rain because his crops required water to grow and produce a good harvest.

Q3. What destroyed Lencho's crops?

Answer: A severe hailstorm destroyed Lencho's entire crop.

Q4. Why did Lencho write a letter to God?

Answer: Lencho wrote a letter to God because his crops had been destroyed and he needed money to support his family.

Q5. How did the postmaster help Lencho?

Answer: The postmaster collected money from his employees and contributed some of his own money. He sent Lencho seventy pesos.

Q6. Why was Lencho angry after receiving the money?

Answer: Lencho had requested one hundred pesos but received only seventy pesos. He suspected that the post office employees had taken the remaining money.

Q7. What is the irony in the story?

Answer: The people who helped Lencho were the same people whom he suspected of stealing his money. This creates irony in the story.

Q8. What message does the story convey?

Answer: The story highlights faith, kindness and generosity. It also shows how misunderstanding can prevent people from recognising the goodness of others.

6. Important Facts

  • Author: Gregorio López y Fuentes.
  • Main character: Lencho.
  • Lencho was a farmer.
  • His crops were destroyed by hailstones.
  • He requested 100 pesos.
  • He received 70 pesos.
  • The postmaster helped him.
  • Irony is an important literary device in the story.
